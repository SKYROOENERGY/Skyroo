Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

A few answers to help you get started. For advice specific to your property, contact SKYROO.

Can switching to solar really make a difference to my electricity bills?

It certainly can! By generating your own electricity from sunlight, you can reduce how much power you need to purchase from the grid. The more solar energy you use in your home, the greater your potential savings. It's a smart way to take more control of your energy costs.

Is a solar battery worth adding to my home?

A battery isn't essential, but it can be a great addition! Instead of sending all your unused solar energy back to the grid, a battery lets you store some of it for later. That means you can make use of your solar power in the evening, too. Whether it's worthwhile depends on your energy usage and household needs.

Will my solar system still be performing years down the track?

Solar panels are built for the long run! Quality panels can continue producing electricity for 25–30 years or more, although their output gradually decreases over time. With the right system and proper care, you can enjoy the benefits of solar for decades.

What happens to my solar power when the weather isn't sunny?

Don't worry, your panels don't need perfect sunshine to work! They can still generate electricity on cloudy or overcast days, just at a reduced capacity. Your home can also draw electricity from the grid when your solar system isn't producing enough.

What if my household needs more solar power in the future?

Your energy needs might change, and your solar setup may be able to grow with you! Depending on your inverter, available roof space and system design, additional panels may be an option. SKYROO can help you explore what's possible for your home.

Will installing solar mean days of disruption at home?

Not usually! A typical residential solar installation can often be completed within one or two days. More complex systems may require additional time. We'll walk you through the process beforehand so you know what to expect.
